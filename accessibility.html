Accessibility Statement

Updated September 28, 2026

JustBaila wants its website, dance instruction, events, and community experiences to be welcoming and usable by people with disabilities.

Our approach

We work toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA and use semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, text alternatives, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and clear form labels. Accessibility is an ongoing process, especially as Underground features evolve.

Need assistance?

If you encounter a barrier, need information in another format, or need an accommodation for a class or event, contact us. Please describe the page, feature, class, or event involved and the support that would help.

Email justbaila@gmail.com or call (805) 570-1974. We will make a good-faith effort to respond and provide a reasonable alternative.

Feedback

Accessibility feedback is welcome. Reporting a problem will not affect your access to JustBaila or Underground.
