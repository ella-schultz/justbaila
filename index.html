About Hector

Hector Sanchez has taught Bachata, Salsa, Cumbia, and more for decades. From a young age, Hector has made a career of performing and teaching a variety of Latin dance styles, including salsa, cha-cha, and merengue, as well as ballroom dance.

Hector creates a space that inspires people to get into dance and gives anyone, regardless of socioeconomic background or ethnicity, a safe environment to perform. Hector strongly believes dancing is a powerful tool for unity and understanding.

Hector specializes in helping students build confidence, musicality, and strong fundamentals in a welcoming and structured environment, whether you are stepping onto the dance floor for the first time or refining advanced technique.
