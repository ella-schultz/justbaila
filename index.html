About Hector

Hector Sanchez has taught Bachata, Salsa, Cumbia and more for decades! From a young age Hector has made a career of performing and teaching a variety of Latin dance styles, including salsa, cha-cha, and merengue, as well as ballroom dance.

Hector hopes to create a space to inspire people to get into dance and have a program where anyone, regardless of socioeconomic background or ethnicity, can perform in a safe environment. Hector strongly believes that dancing is a powerful tool for creating unity and understanding.

Hector specializes in helping students build confidence, musicality, and strong fundamentals in a welcoming and structured environment. Whether you're stepping onto the dance floor for the first time or refining advanced technique, Hector's teaching style focuses on clear instruction, strong fundamentals, and practical social dance skills.
